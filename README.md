# googlePanicImages | googlePanicImage
Use googlePanicImage to display a direct 'View' link, 'image resolution/dimensions' and 'size in byte/kb or mb" on hovered images at Google Image search.

Also additional options are provided to configure and show image sizes directly on the images of the search or hide images texts.

## Working/non-working state of the addon/extension
Due to changes in Google search when being logged in with a Google Account, the addon is partially broken - I am aware of that.
Please use the issue https://github.com/jrie/googlePanicImage/issues/19 in order to track changes.

If used without a Google account, everything is working fine!
